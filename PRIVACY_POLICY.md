## English

### 1. Introduction

This policy describes how the mobile application **"Shopping List — BuyList"** (hereinafter referred to as the "App") processes user data in accordance with RuStore, Google Play, AppGallery requirements, and applicable data protection laws.

Developer: **Dzmitry Zalenski**. Contact email: **dz529770@gmail.com**.

### 2. Data Collection

The App operates primarily in offline mode. All shopping lists, interface settings, archives, and discount card data are stored **exclusively locally** on the user's device.

**We do NOT collect:**
- Personal identification data (full name, phone, address).
- Bank card data.
- Contacts from the phone book.

**What is used on the device:**
- **Voice input** — processed by the device's built-in Android speech recognition service (Google, Samsung, Huawei, etc.). Depending on device settings and available offline models, audio may be processed locally or transmitted to the respective provider's servers for recognition. The App itself does not store or transmit voice data.
- **Discount card photos** — saved locally in the app's internal storage.
- **Yandex Advertising SDK (Yandex Mobile Ads)** — may collect the device's advertising identifier (OAID/ADID), approximate location, and device information (model, OS version) for displaying personalized and contextual advertisements. Data processing is governed by Yandex's privacy policy.

### 3. Data Usage

Collected data is used strictly for the functioning of the App:
- Displaying and managing shopping lists.
- Operating the local dish database (ingredient suggestions without internet).
- Maintaining an archive of completed purchases.
- Storing digital copies of discount cards.

**List export:** users may voluntarily export a shopping list to a file and share it via messengers or email. This action is performed only upon explicit user choice.

### 4. Sharing with Third Parties

- Shopping list data is **NOT automatically shared** with third parties.
- The Yandex Advertising SDK processes the device advertising identifier independently under its own policy.
- Export files are shared only with apps and contacts **manually selected by the user**.

### 5. Storage and Security

- All data is stored in the app's **internal Android storage** and is inaccessible to other apps (starting from Android 11 — scoped storage).
- The dish database operates entirely **without an internet connection**.
- Standard Android platform-level security measures are applied (storage encryption on modern devices).
- When the app is uninstalled, all local data is deleted along with it.

### 6. User Rights

The user can at any time:
- Delete items from the shopping list.
- Clear the current shopping list.
- Delete archived lists.
- Delete saved discount cards.
- Change the language and UI style.
- Disable ad display (if such an option is available in the app).

### 7. Children

The app is intended for a general audience, does not contain 18+ content, and **does not specifically collect children's data**. Advertising materials are filtered in accordance with ad network policies.

We may update this privacy policy. The current version is always available at:
- **https://github.com/dz529770-a11y/my-app-privacy-policy/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md**

The GitHub page above always contains the most up-to-date version.

### 2. 数据收集

本应用主要在离线模式下运行。所有购物清单、界面设置、存档和折扣卡数据均 **仅存储在用户的设备本地**。

**我们不会收集：**
- 个人身份信息（姓名、电话、地址）。
- 银行卡数据。
- 手机通讯录中的联系人。

**设备上使用的内容：**
- **语音输入** — 由设备内置的 Android 语音识别服务（Google、Samsung、Huawei 等）处理。根据设备设置和离线模型可用性，音频可能在本地处理或传输到相应提供商的服务器进行识别。本应用本身不存储或传输语音数据。
- **折扣卡照片** — 保存在应用内部存储中。
- **Yandex 广告 SDK (Yandex Mobile Ads)** — 可能会收集设备的广告标识符 (OAID/ADID)、大致位置和设备信息（型号、操作系统版本）以展示个性化和相关广告。数据处理受 Yandex 隐私政策约束。

### 3. 数据使用

收集的数据严格用于应用的正常运行：
- 显示和管理购物清单。
- 运行本地菜品数据库（无需网络即可提供食材建议）。
- 维护已完成的购买记录存档。
- 存储折扣卡的数字副本。

**导出清单：** 用户可以主动将购物清单导出为文件，并通过即时通讯工具或电子邮件分享。此操作仅在用户明确选择时执行。

### 4. 向第三方共享数据

- 购物清单数据 **不会自动共享** 给第三方。
- Yandex 广告 SDK 根据其自身政策独立处理设备广告标识符。
- 导出文件仅共享给 **用户手动选择** 的应用程序和联系人。

### 5. 存储与安全

- 所有数据存储在应用的 **Android 内部存储** 中，其他应用无法访问（自 Android 11 起采用分区存储）。
- 菜品数据库完全 **无需网络连接** 即可运行。
- 采用标准的 Android 平台级安全措施（现代设备上的存储加密）。
- 卸载应用时，所有本地数据将一并删除。

### 6. 用户权利

用户可以随时：
- 删除购物清单中的项目。
- 清空当前购物清单。
- 删除已存档的清单。
- 删除保存的折扣卡。
- 更改语言和界面样式。
- 禁用广告展示（如应用提供此选项）。
